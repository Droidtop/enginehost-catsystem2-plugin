# Research attribution

The engine - the KIF archive and key layer, the CST script reader, the HG-3
image decoder and the scene player - was written for enginehost from the
publicly documented archive layout, CatScene structure, line semantics and image
packing described by
[TriggersTools.CatSystem2](https://github.com/trigger-segfault/TriggersTools.CatSystem2)
(MIT). No source code from that project or from the proprietary CatSystem2
runtime is included here.

src/cp932_table.h is the Shift-JIS to Unicode mapping, generated by
tools/gen_cp932.py from Python's own cp932 codec. src/blowfish_tables.h is
Blowfish's published initial state, the hexadecimal digits of pi.

SDL2 and SDL2_ttf are used under the zlib licence and are not vendored here.

Games, CatSystem2, and their assets are not redistributed by this repository.
